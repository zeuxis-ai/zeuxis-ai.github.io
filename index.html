01 · The problem

The first weeks

The most important decisions are made first, and fast.

In the first weeks, a building gets its shape. Under time pressure, the easiest thing to measure is profit. So profit wins.

What is decided

Height, depth, gaps, how much of the plot stays open.

The massing. Set in a few weeks, it stays for 60 to 100 years.

What is measured

Floor area, flats, yield.

Spreadsheets answer this in minutes. Daylight, noise or carbon take studies that arrive months later.

What follows

Dark flats, loud bedrooms, wasted material.

By the time the other numbers arrive, the shape is fixed and changing it is expensive.
