What it does
01

Reads the documents

The brief, the zoning code, the preservation constraints, the noise and cost limits — several hundred hard rules, extracted as constraints rather than typed into a parameter form.

02

Generates every volume that satisfies them

Real typologies with real roofs — Zeile, Blockrand, Punktbau, Kamm, Grossform — at the dimensions the rules and the plan actually permit. Exhaustively, because that is the only way to know nothing viable was missed.

03

Simulates the top one percent properly

Cheap filters cut the field to the promising percent. Those go through real solvers — energy, daylight, cost — on watertight geometry. Not a proxy score.

04

Throws almost all of it away

What arrives is a handful, opened up: circulation, unit mix, and the evidence of what each one survived. Every discarded scheme is inspectable, with the reason it was cut.

Position

We do not design the building. We find the few worth looking at.

So the architect starts there on day one instead of month three.

An architect does not want a bigger search space. They want a smaller one. Choice is the cost, not the benefit — a hundred million compliant volumes, or even five hundred, just moves the triage onto the person you were meant to help. Generation is the mechanism. Reduction is the product.

The name

Zeuxis was commissioned by the city of Croton to paint Helen, the most beautiful woman in the world. He could not find a single model who had all of it, so he chose five and composed one image from the best of each. Note what the story actually says: Helen already existed, distributed across the women of Croton. He did not paint five hundred faces — he went out, looked at everyone, and came back with five. The search was the work. The five was the deliverable. Our system does the looking.
